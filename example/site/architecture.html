This site's own architecture

This site is itself an archreator project. Its EA layers, below, are the concrete "what does a filled-in docs/ea/ look like" answer — including its one AI actor, modeled with a stated autonomy level rather than left implicit. Every diagram and table links to the canonical source in the repo.

Business: actors and roles

Who acts on this project — and, for the AI actor, exactly how much it may do on its own.

ActorKindAutonomyDecision rightsEscalation path
Pilot Human — Approves/merges any change; sole authority over Pages/repo settings —
Copilot AI Co-pilot May draft and commit changes to site/docs within example/, open a PR. No merge rights, no settings access Opens a PR to Pilot; on a Principle conflict, stops and surfaces it instead of proceeding
Template adopter Human, external — None — read-only visitor —
